# DealTrace — Vehicle Deals & Valuation Comparison Platform

DealTrace is an intelligent platform designed for dealerships, wholesale buyers, and automotive teams to track car deals and make data-driven buying decisions by comparing prospective vehicle purchases against historical transactions.

## Key Features
- **VIN Auto-Decoder**: Instant specification extraction via the official NHTSA VPIC API.
- **Historical Comparison Engine**: Automatically matches prospective cars against past deals of the same Year, Make, and Model.
- **Side-by-Side Analytics**: Visual breakdown of purchase price, mileage, repair costs, total investment, sale price, and net profit margins.
- **Team Lessons Learned**: Surfacing past inspection notes and mechanical watchouts for specific models.
- **Supabase Admin Authentication**: Restricting deal records and valuation data strictly to authorized dealership administrators.
- **PDF Exporting**: One-click professional deal summary and comparison reports.

## Getting Started
1. Open `login.html` in your web browser.
2. Sign in with your dealership administrator credentials.
3. Access the deal workspace (`add-car.html`) to decode VINs, evaluate new purchases, and log completed deals.
